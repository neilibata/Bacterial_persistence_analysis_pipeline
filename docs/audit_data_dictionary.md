# Audited cohort metadata — data dictionary

Two files accompany the data-hygiene follow-up:

| file | rows | what it is |
|---|---|---|
| `metadata_audited.csv` | 18,306 | every original metadata column unchanged, plus the `qc_*` columns below |
| `study_audit_table.csv` | 84 | one row per study: cohort size, per-field completeness, per-flag counts |

Both are regenerated by `tools/build_audited_metadata.py`; neither is hand-edited.

Samples that failed audit are **present and flagged, never removed**. A filtered
file cannot document the errors it hides, and documenting them is the point. The
three isolate genomes excluded from the analysis cohort are re-added here for
exactly that reason.

---

## Two severities, and why they are not one number

A study whose submitter typed the host species where a metagenome term belonged
is not in the same position as one carrying amplicon libraries. Collapsing both
into a single "flagged" count misrepresents both: CosteaPI 2017 reads **100%
flagged** on the combined measure and **0.4%** on the one that bears on whether
its data can be used.

| column | n | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `qc_exclude_recommended` | 162 | the data is not usable for a community analysis |
| `qc_record_issue` | 1,280 | the record is wrong; the data may be perfectly sound |

`qc_flags` carries the individual flag names, semicolon-separated.
`qc_any_flag` is the union and is provided only for convenience — for anything
quantitative, use the two columns above rather than their union.

## Measured — from the alignment, not the archive

Computed from the breadth and depth matrices and keyed on the matrix index,
which is the pipeline's own sample linkage. Unambiguous.

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `richness_10pct` | SGBs detected at ≥10% breadth. Cohort median 155. |
| `median_breadth_pct` | median genome fraction covered, across covered SGBs |
| `dpcb_median` | depth per covered base. ~1.8 for shotgun; pileup pushes it up. |
| `max_breadth`, `top1_share`, `n_sgb_any` | supporting detail |

`dpcb_median` is the discriminating quantity. Random shotgun ties breadth to
depth — at genome-wide depth *d* expected breadth is 1 − e^−*d* — so depth over
the covered bases sits near 1×. Amplicon reads break that by piling onto one
conserved window.

## Declared — from ENA

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `qc_declared_strategy` | `library_strategy` as deposited (WGS, AMPLICON, …) |
| `qc_declared_scientific_name` | `scientific_name` as deposited |
| `qc_archive_bases` | summed `base_count` over the sample's runs |
| `qc_all_runs_resolved` | true when *every* recorded run was found in ENA |

Joined by the documented multi-key rule — `BioSample`, `SRS ID`,
`Chosen_match_ID`, `SRA_Accession` — with runs gathered from `SRA_Accession`
**and** `Secondary_run_IDs`, split on both `;` and `,`. Resolves 17,623 of
18,306 (96.3%). Blank where nothing resolves, rather than guessed.

## Accuracy flags

### Data does not match the declaration

| flag | n | definition |
|---|---|---|
| `qc_amplicon_suspect` | 105 | `median_breadth_pct` < 0.02% **and** `dpcb_median` > 2.5 |
| `qc_strategy_contradicted` | 105 | amplicon signature **and** declared WGS |
| `qc_isolate_like` | 4 | `richness_10pct` ≤ 3 **and** `top1_share` ≥ 0.3 |
| `qc_low_richness` | 104 | fewer than 5 SGBs at 10% breadth |

**All 105 amplicon-signature samples are declared WGS.** Not most — every one.
Both thresholds are empirical, from all 24,173 samples: `dpcb_median` has a
cohort median of 1.77 and is flat at 1.68–1.99 across every breadth band, and the
elevated subpopulation exists only below 0.02% breadth, where the 90th percentile
is 6.6–10.7 against ~2.25 in every band above.

Both conditions are required. Deep, well-structured samples routinely exceed the
ratio alone — the deepest sample in Carter 2023 sits at 4.98× and is sound.

### `qc_label_misleading` — read this one carefully

**556 samples (3.04%).** A non-metagenome `scientific_name` on a sample whose
data is an ordinary community (≥20 SGBs detected).

This flag is a **labelling convention issue, not a data quality issue**, and it
should not be read as casting doubt on the samples. It is dominated by
submitters registering gut metagenomes under `Homo sapiens`; the affected samples
are WGS and metagenomic throughout. Whether that constitutes an *error* or an
accepted *convention* is a judgement, and reasonable people will differ.

It is reported because the converse matters: acting on the archive's organism
label alone would have removed 736 sound samples while keeping the three isolate
genomes it failed to distinguish. The label is unreliable in both directions.

For the unambiguous case, use `qc_strategy_contradicted` (0.57%), where the
declaration and the data cannot both be true.

### Record disagrees with itself

| flag | n | definition |
|---|---|---|
| `qc_disease_not_rolled_up` | 410 | a `disease_*` flag set while `Diseased (all)` is not |
| `qc_depth_disagrees_archive` | 136 | metadata `Total bases` differs >1.5× from the archive's own count |
| `qc_health_contradiction` | 49 | `Healthy` and `Unhealthy` both asserted |
| `qc_duplicate_id` | 42 | `Chosen_match_ID` shared by more than one row |
| `qc_bmi_obesity_mismatch` | 7 | BMI ≥ 30 without an obesity flag, or < 25 with one |
| `qc_bmi_implausible` | 1 | BMI outside 8–90 |

`qc_depth_disagrees_archive` fires only where `qc_all_runs_resolved` is true.
Without that guard the archive total is short by construction and the flag would
measure join quality rather than the data.

## Completeness

`study_audit_table.csv` carries `pct_<field>` per study. Cohort-wide:

| field | complete | studies with none |
|---|---|---|
| Country, Lifestyle, Total bases, accessions | 100.0% | 0 |
| Age group | 96.3% | 2 |
| Gender | 87.3% | 17 |
| Age | 69.4% | **27** |
| BMI | 62.6% | **37** |

The "studies with none" column is the more informative one. Completeness is
bimodal: a study generally either reports a field or omits it entirely, so a
cohort average of 62.6% describes almost no individual study. Lloyd-Price 2019
is the clearest case — 1,125 samples at 99.6% Age and 100% Gender, and 0% BMI.

Every field at 100% is one the archive generates. Every field below 90% is one a
submitter must type.
